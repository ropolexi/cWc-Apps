Step 2 of 5

Store your recovery words on this browser?

This is optional. Saving the recovery words in this browser’s Local Storage lets this chainWare page reopen the wallet on this browser without making you paste the recovery words every time.

For now: chainWare is currently an experiment. This risk may not matter much if this wallet contains only experimental coins and nothing you care about losing, so you may decide convenience is more useful right now. Do not rely on browser storage for a wallet that later holds anything valuable. Keep a separate copy of your recovery words.
Step 3 of 5

1,000 free cWc

chainWare is an experiment, currently, and therefore the first 100 eligible newly registered wallets receive 1,000 cWc so people can test the network and use its features. The reward is determined by the protocol, not by this webpage.

Those coins are Layer 1 spendable funds. You can use some of them to fund Layer 2 storage so you can create profiles, posts, comments, images and videos.
